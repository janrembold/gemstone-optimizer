## External comparison — deliberately not calibrated

[Brilliani's published T57 page](https://www.brillianilabs.com/registry/t57) and [rectangle comparison](https://brillianilabs.com/registry/cuts/rectangle) report diamond useful return 92.28%, leak 1.88%, fire 78.85 and Global 88.61 for their T57. Our figures above do not reproduce those external scores. Different full geometry, Fresnel energy versus a ray census, residual internal energy, dispersion and perceptual normalization prevent a controlled numerical agreement claim. We have not adjusted constants to fit their ratings. Validation against analytic physics succeeds; experimental gemstone light-performance validation is still outstanding.
